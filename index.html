My Reflection

"I consider myself a responsible and committed English learner. As a student of a bachelor’s degree in foreign Languages, English is very important for my academic and professional future. My strongest skill is reading because I can understand academic texts more easily. However, my speaking is weaker than my reading, and I sometimes feel insecure when I speak. SMART Goals

Goal 1: Vocabulary improvement

▼
  • Specific: Learn 50 new academic words related to my field.
  • Measurable: Weekly quiz using flashcards.
  • Achievable: Study 10 words per day, 5 days a week.
  • Relevant: Essential for reading academic papers.
  • Time-bound: Achieve this by the end of the month.

Goal 2: Grammar improvement

▼
  • Specific: Master the use of conditional sentences (Type 1 & 2).
  • Measurable: Complete 10 grammar exercises with 90% accuracy.
  • Achievable: Watch 2 tutorial videos and practice with online tools.
  • Relevant: Necessary for more complex writing and speaking.
  • Time-bound: Complete within the next two weeks.

Goal 3: Speaking fluency

▼
  • Specific: Participate in at least one conversation club session.
  • Measurable: Speak for at least 5 minutes during the session.
  • Achievable: Prepare common topics in advance to feel more confident.
  • Relevant: Overcoming insecurity is my biggest challenge.
  • Time-bound: Do this by next Friday.
